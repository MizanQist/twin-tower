The concept

Where the citymeets the deck

  • 2Towers

The proposal places two identical twelve-storey towers at the two ends of a single podium, 100 metres long and 40 metres deep, on Plot 765. The ground floor is one shared room for the whole scheme: double-height lobbies under each tower, four retail units along the avenue arcade, the car-park entry and the back of house. Above it, between the towers, an open-air deck carries the amenities; beside it, behind glass, the gyms.

Floors one to five are offices, three suites of 300, 200 and 150 square metres around a compact core. Floors six to eleven are homes: one three-bedroom and two two-bedroom apartments a floor, each with a separate kitchen and a recessed balcony. One basement parks 130 cars.

        Design

        Architecture

        Champagne-bronze fins over clear glass, a glazed two-storey base and an open metal crown: two compact towers drawn as one pair on a podium four tower-widths long.

        The towers are twice as tall as they are wide; the podium is one storey; everything between them is open air. The fins give the glass its depth, and the crown gives the skyline a line to finish on.

        A joint venture on Plot 765, Central Area

        • Wrace Group, Abuja.

          Wrace Group is an Abuja developer with a portfolio across real estate, agriculture, energy and media. It is bringing a fifteen-storey office block to Constitution Avenue, opposite the World Trade Centre, and proposes this scheme as its next address in the Central Business District.

        • The land comes in; the building comes out.

          The landowner contributes Plot 765. Wrace Group designs, funds and builds the scheme and manages its delivery. The completed building, or the income from it, is shared in a ratio to be agreed between the parties before the design is developed.
